Education, aspirations, skills, and the things that keep me curious.

📚 Educational Background

I am currently a first-year undergraduate student pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at the University of Technology. My coursework has covered foundational and advanced topics including:

  • Data Structures & Algorithms
  • Web Application Development
  • Database Systems & SQL
  • Object-Oriented Programming (Java & Python)
  • Human-Computer Interaction
  • Software Engineering Principles

Prior to university I completed secondary education with distinction in Mathematics, Physics, and Computer Studies, which sparked my interest in technology and problem-solving.

🎯 Career Aspirations & Goals

My long-term goal is to become a skilled full-stack developer who builds products that are both technically solid and genuinely useful to people. In the next few years I aim to:

  1. Complete my degree with a strong academic record and a portfolio of real projects.
  2. Secure an internship or junior developer role focused on front-end or full-stack web development.
  3. Contribute to open-source projects and continuously improve my craft through deliberate practice.
  4. Eventually specialize in accessible, performant user interfaces and developer tooling.

I believe that clear code, thoughtful design, and continuous learning are the foundations of a successful career in software.
